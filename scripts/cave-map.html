HOW TO READ THE ATLAS

Follow the passages

Route labels give the departure room and command: #8: D means DOWN from room 8. Follow the arrowhead to the destination. Return directions are listed separately. U / D mean up / down. MA / MD identify the collapsed mazes.

X marks conditional motions; conditions are hidden. Purple dashed lines are magic. Dashed room boxes continue in another numbered panel.

Choose a room to see its description and complete travel alternatives. The two mazes are collapsed, with their individual locations available here.

This map does not track your game or change its state. The repository is reached when the cave closes.
